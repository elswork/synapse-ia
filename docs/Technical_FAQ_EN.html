Strategy for Obtaining the .IA ccTLD

To: Hellenic Organization for Standardization (ELOT)

(Note for ELOT: This document is drafted in English to facilitate direct forwarding to ISO (Geneva) and IANA services, ensuring accuracy of technical terms.)
Q1: What is the purpose of requesting an additional Alpha-2 code ('IA') if Greece already possesses '.gr'?
The '.gr' code is the general sovereignty identifier. The 'IA' code, specifically linked to the Innovation and Heritage District of Antikythera, functions as a technological specialty domain. Given its coincidence with "Artificial Intelligence" (Inteligencia Artificial) and high market value, it grants Greece a global competitive advantage.
Q2: Is it administratively viable to request the 'IA' code from ISO?
It is fully viable through the "Exceptional Reservation" mechanism (ISO 3166 MA). Precedents such as .eu and .uk operate under this status. The 'IA' code is currently free and has no assignment conflicts.
Q3: What is the technical and cultural justification?
The foundation is World Technological Heritage. Antikythera is the origin of analog computing. The 'IA' code allows Greece to claim sovereignty over the historical narrative of computing.

Q4: How is the delegation process managed with IANA/ICANN?
The process is hierarchical:
  1. National Petition (ELOT -> ISO)
  2. Inclusion in Exceptional Reservation List
  3. Technical Delegation (IANA)
Q5: How are stability and security guaranteed?
Under DNSSEC standards and state governance. The State can delegate technical operation to a registry with proven capacity, ensuring a secure environment for technology companies.
Q6: What is the projected direct economic impact?
Following the benchmark of Anguilla (.ai) (+20% national revenue), a multi-million recurring revenue is estimated to fund the repopulation of Antikythera at no cost to the general budget.
